# Physical Game Materials & Unplugged Classroom Activities

> **Unplugged Learning Module for Grades 4–5**

---

## 🎯 Overview

Before students touch a computer screen or write code in Scratch, they play **The Five-Head Story-Generation Game** physically. 

The physical game transforms abstract mathematical concepts into tactile objects:
- **Tokens** are physical cards on a table.
- **Context** is a physical sliding cardboard window framing the last 3 cards.
- **The Five Heads** are 5 students in a group, each holding their designated scoring sheet and vocabulary checklist.
- **Sampling** is performed with a physical 10-sided die ($d10$).

---

## 📦 Planned Physical Components

1. **Word Card Deck**:
   - 120 unique vocabulary cards representing all tokens in the children's story corpus.
   - Distinct color-coded borders for Character words (Blue), Setting words (Green), Action words (Orange), and Pivot/Grammar words (White).
2. **Scoring Sheets & Badges**:
   - *Head 1: Grammar Scout Badge* & Bigram/Trigram tally sheets.
   - *Head 2: Character Keeper Badge* & Protagonist word banks (Milo, Pip, Zara).
   - *Head 3: Setting Painter Badge* & Location word banks (Forest, Castle, Cave).
   - *Head 4: Action Booster Badge* & Action/Sensory word list.
   - *Head 5: Repeat Blocker Badge* & Clothesline of recent tokens with clothespins.
3. **d10 Probability Strip**:
   - A 10-slot whiteboard strip where students write candidate names across slots 1 to 10 using the Hamilton Largest Remainder Method.
4. **10-Sided Dice ($d10$)**:
   - Fair 10-sided physical polyhedral dice numbered 1–10.
